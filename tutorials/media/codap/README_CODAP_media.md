## Text version of the video (the accessible alternative)

1. File → Import… → Local File, then drop compas_classroom.csv on the box (or click the box to choose it)
2. Click Graph in the toolbar
3. Drag the c_charge_degree column heading to the bottom of the graph (“Drag an attribute or click here”)
4. Click the graph, open Config on the right, and check Fuse Points into Bars (Count)
5. Open Measure and check Count; the totals 1949 and 1051 appear above the bars
6. Drag the two_year_recid column heading into the middle of the graph (not the left edge)
7. Hover over the orange part of each bar to read the rate: F 983 of 1949 (50.4%), M 382 of 1051 (36.3%)

## Alt text for each image

- **codap_step1.png:** CODAP's Import Data window with Local File selected and a box labeled 'Drop file here or click here to select a file.'
- **codap_step2.png:** CODAP with the compas_classroom table on the left and a new, empty graph of dots on the right. The Graph button in the toolbar is highlighted.
- **codap_step3.png:** The graph now splits the dots into two columns labeled F and M, with c_charge_degree on the horizontal axis.
- **codap_step4.png:** The Configuration menu beside the graph with 'Fuse Points into Bars' checked and Count selected. The graph shows two bars, F taller than M.
- **codap_step5.png:** The Measure menu with Count checked. The numbers 1949 and 1051 appear above the F and M bars.
- **codap_step6.png:** The bars are now colored: orange for Recidivated at the bottom and purple for Survived on top. A legend under the graph names both colors.
- **codap_step7.png:** A tooltip over the orange part of the F bar reads: 983 of 1949 F (50.4%) are Recidivated.
- **codap_warmup_storyboard.png:** Seven numbered screenshots of the CODAP steps above, arranged in two columns, ending with a box headed 'Check your graph': F 1,949 people, 983 Recidivated (50.4%); M 1,051 people, 382 Recidivated (36.3%); Recidivated means rearrested within two years and Survived means not rearrested.
